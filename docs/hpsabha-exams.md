## 1. The organisation (PRIMARY — dbhpscentral.org)

- **Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha, Madras** (spelled "Dakshin
  Bharath Hindi Prachar Sabha" on the site header).
- Founded **1918 by Mahatma Gandhi**; HQ **T. Nagar, Chennai – 600017**.
- **Declared by Parliament as an Institution of National Importance
  by Act 14 of 1964.**
- Exams are **recognised by the Central and State Governments**.
- **~4 lakh students** write the examinations in a year.
- Also conducts Hindi **Typewriting** (Lower, Higher & High Speed)
  and **Shorthand** in Hindi.
- Wings named on the Examination page: Examination Department,
  Finance wing (fees of Pracharaks / Non-Pracharaks / students updated
  daily on the web application), Pathrika wing (publishes magazines
  *Samachar* monthly and a quarterly; model question papers, fee
  structure and articles for pracharaks).
- Contact (PRIMARY, contactus.html): **Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar
  Sabha, Chennai, T Nagar, Chennai – 600017. Phone 044-2434 1824.
  Exams: 044-2433 2338 / +91 8124333004. Website www.dbhpscentral.org.**
  (Secondary listings also give manager@dbhpscentral.org and
  adminmanager@dbhpscentral.org — not confirmed on the official pages
  read.)

## 5. Eligibility, direct entry & exemptions (PRIMARY — feestru.php)

- **General eligibility (SECONDARY guide, consistent with the
  official "no minimum qualification, Class IV upwards" line):**
  open to all ages, from Class IV upwards, no minimum qualification
  required. (The official pages read do not state an age/class rule
  directly; the "Class IV upwards" line comes from the secondary
  guide and older DBHPS material — flag as secondary.)
- **Direct Rashtrabhasha (exemption from Madhyama)** — any one of:
  attended SSLC / MATRIC / SSC exam with Hindi subject; studied
  8th class in Kendriya Vidyalaya / Navodaya Vidyalaya / CBSE with
  Hindi and passed the whole exam, or passed only Hindi with 50%;
  passed 7th or 8th public exam with Hindi as first language or
  50% in Hindi only; passed SSC or junior Cambridge with Hindi or
  50% in Hindi only; university degree holder / Hindi oriental title
  holder; Central Government Prabodha exam passed; Central Govt
  recognised institution equivalent exam passed; Hindi-medium
  4th Std passed; Central Hindi Directorate Parichay exam passed;
  passed 9th standard with Hindi as second language.
  *Note: educational qualification documents are compulsory; for the
  9th-std second-language case a certificate from the principal/HM is
  required.*
- **Direct Praveshika (exemption from Rashtrabhasha)** — any one of:
  passed SSLC / SSC / MATRIC / ASC / junior Cambridge with Hindi;
  passed Central Government Praveen examination; Central Govt
  recognised voluntary Hindi institution equivalent exam passed;
  passed 9th std in a Hindi-medium school; passed 9th in a Central
  or Navodaya school; passed Karnataka Government Vidhwan exam.
- **Direct Visharad Poorvardh (exemption from Praveshika)** — any
  one of: passed 10th / senior Cambridge / +2 with Hindi as first
  language; passed Hindi-medium matriculation exam (further items
  cut off in the fetched copy — see fee page for the full list).
- **Direct Praveen Poorvardh (exemption from Visharad)** — passed a
  degree and taken the subject for two years from a recognised
  university.
- **Easy Paper eligibility:** school/college principal/head or a
  Gazetted-rank officer must certify the applicant has **not studied
  the regional language** in school/college. The "Easy Language"
  3rd paper replaces the regional-language paper: Telugu / Kannada /
  Tamil / Malayalam / Marathi Bhasha Bodhini I, II, III.
- **Exemption validity:** once obtained, an exemption is valid for
  up to **two years**. Exemption applications must be submitted with
  the examination application. Easy-language answer booklets are
  cancelled if the exemption was not obtained. For Easy Language
  candidates, **only the Hindi subject marks count for the grade**.

## 6. Pass criteria (SECONDARY — not on the official pages read)

The official pages fetched do **not** publish pass marks, paper
durations or division rules. The figures below come from the
hindustanitongue.com DBHPS 2026 guide and match what our code
already says — treat as secondary until confirmed against an
official source.

| Level | To pass | First class |
|---|---|---|
| Parichaya | 30% overall | 50% overall |
| Prathamik | 30% minimum in each paper | 50% in each paper |
| Madhyama | 70 marks total AND 30% in each paper | 120 marks total AND 50% in each paper |
| Rashtrabhasha | 70 marks total AND 30% in each paper | 120 marks total AND 50% in each paper |
| Praveshika and above | not stated in the guide read | not stated |

**Parichaya paper blueprint (SECONDARY — coaching Instagram reel,
unverified):** pass needs 35 out of 45 marks; sections — Missing
letters (5), See the picture & write its name (10), Opposites (5),
Number Names (5), Meanings (10), Poem (5), Match (5). First class
needs 60 out of 65 marks, adding "See the picture & answer the
questions" (10) and "Translate into English" (10). The two different
totals (45 vs 65) suggest different paper versions; do not build the
pack on this alone — use it only as a shape hint, and prefer the
official "Hindi Parichay" book + the app's own beginner structure.

## 8. Equivalents (what each level counts as)

- **PRIMARY (official):** the examinations are recognised by the
  Central and State Governments (Examination page). No
  level-by-level equivalence table is published on the official
  pages read.
- **SECONDARY (guides, consistent with our code):** Parichaya =
  Hindi awareness; Prathamik = primary level; Madhyama = middle
  school Hindi; Rashtrabhasha = high school Hindi; Praveshika =
  pre-degree; Rashtrabhasha Visharad = graduate (BA) level;
  Rashtrabhasha Praveen = post-graduate level. Treat as guide
  consensus, not an official statement.

## 11. Discrepancies vs our app (`lib/data/exam_packs.dart`)

Read 2026-11-18. "Our code" = the ladder as committed.

1. **WRONG SESSION RULE in our code comment.** Our file says
   "Exams twice a year (Feb for Madras City, Aug/Sep elsewhere)".
   Official: Parichaya is **January only, Madras City only**;
   Prathamic–Praveshika are February (Madras) / August–September
   (all states); Higher exams run February AND August/September in
   all states. **Fix the comment.**
2. **SPELLING.** Our note says the Sabha spells them "Prathamik /
   Madhyama / Rashtrabhasha". The official pages actually write
   **"Prathamic"** and **"Rashtrabasha"** most often (and
   "Pravesika", "Uttrarardh" with typos). Secondary guides use
   Prathamik/Rashtrabhasha. The app should keep the Sabha spelling
   as shown on the official site with spoken aliases — our alias
   approach is right, but the "Sabha spelling" claim in the comment
   should say Prathamic/Rashtrabasha as printed on
   dbhpscentral.org. **Fix the comment.**
3. **PARICHAYA book.** Our code lists `Dakshin-1 (Subodh Hindi
   Patamala)` for Parichaya. The official Books page puts
   "Saral Hindi Bodhini (Dakshin-1)" under **Prathamic**, and the
   Feb 2021/2022 syllabus lists for Parichaya just say "Hindi
   Parichay". **Fix: Parichaya book = "Hindi Parichay"; move
   Saral Hindi Bodhini (Dakshin-1) to Prathamik.**
4. **PRATHAMIK books.** Our code lists 2 books (Dakshin-2 +
   Rachana). Official: **3 books** — Saral Hindi Bodhini
   (Dakshin-1), Subodh Hindi Patamala-1 (Dakshin-2), Subodh Hindi
   Rachana-1 (Dakshin-3). **Fix the list.**
5. **MADHYAMA books.** Our code lists `Dakshin-3, Bharat-1,
   Rachana-1`. Official: **Bharat-1** (Subodh Hindi Patamala-2),
   **Bharat-2** (Hindi Katha Sarovar), **Bharat-3** (Subodh Hindi
   Rachana-2). "Dakshin-3" belongs to Prathamik, and "Rachana-1"
   is the Prathamik book. **Fix the list.**
6. **RASHTRABHASHA books.** Our code lists only
   `Subodh Hindi Patamala-3 (Hindi-1)`. Official: **3 books** —
   Hindi-1 (Patamala-3), Hindi-2 (Katha Ratnavali), Hindi-3
   (Rachana-3). **Fix the list.**
7. **PRAVESHIKA / VISHARAD / PRAVEEN books.** Our code has
   placeholder strings ("DBHPS … prescribed texts"). The official
   Books page gives the full per-paper lists — see §7 above.
   **Replace the placeholders with the real lists.**
8. **PAPER COUNTS — all match.** 1/2/2/2/3/3/3/3/3. No change.
9. **PASS CRITERIA — match secondary sources, not verified on
   official pages.** Our "30% in each paper (Prathmik); Madhyama

10. **LEVEL NAMES — match in substance.** Official higher levels
